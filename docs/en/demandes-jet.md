**What it's for:** the GM asks players for a roll from the chat, or pits several characters against each other, without anyone having to look for the right field on their sheet.

**Example:** die button in the chat controls → **Simple roll** → **Stealth**, difficulty `-20`, check two characters → **Send**. Their players click **Roll** on the chat card.

## Nothing to prepare

The window offers every field of your character templates that has **Roll on label click**. That very roll is the one used, with its formula, comparison, criticals and roll window. See [Dice rolls](#jets).

## The request window

The die button in the chat controls (GM):

- **Roll**: the field to roll.
- **Difficulty**: optional. `-20` makes the roll harder, `+20` easier. It shows on the card and applies to the roll's threshold (without a comparison, it changes nothing).
- **Several sheet templates**: they don't all have the same rolls. A character whose sheet lacks the chosen roll is greyed out ("lacks this roll"); "All player characters" only targets those who have it (the count is shown, for example `3/5`).

## Simple roll

"All player characters", or check the ones you want.

- The card is only whispered to their players (and the GM), and reminds who it is for.
- The roll is made on the targeted character.
- The GM can roll for a character by selecting its token.

## Opposed roll

Check at least two tokens on the scene.

- **Defender roll** (optional): if chosen (Attack against Dodge), each token is set to Attack or Defense; otherwise, everyone makes the same roll.
- **Winner**: the highest total, the lowest total, or the **best success** (a success beats a failure, a critical success beats a success, then the biggest margin; if nobody succeeds, nobody wins).

NPCs roll right away; each player rolls from the card, and the others see "Waiting…". A single card shows every participant with their dice and result. When everyone has rolled, the winner is crowned, or the card announces "Tie" or "Nobody wins".

## Going further

Request cards have their own type, **Roll requests**, in the chat messages manager: accent color, icon and CSS.
