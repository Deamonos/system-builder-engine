**What it's for:** giving, selling or bartering items and money between characters, in play, without going through the GM.

**Example:** right-click a dagger in the inventory → **Trade item** → the character in front of you. They accept; a window opens for both, each adds what they want, each validates, done.

## The settings

**System settings**: **Enable the trade system** (checked by default), then **Trades** → **Configure trades**:

- **Tradeable families**: the workshop templates whose items can be traded ("Weapon", "Potion").
- **Exceptions**: specific items that are never traded, dragged into the area provided.
- **Money**: the currencies declared in [Merchants](#marchands). Without a currency, no money in trades.
- **Maximum distance** between tokens (0 = none).
- **Sounds**: request, done, cancelled, for both participants.

## How it goes

1. **Trade item** only appears on a tradeable item.
2. The recipient is a player character whose player is connected, and within range. The GM can start a trade from an NPC.
3. The recipient **accepts** or **refuses**.
4. A two-column window: **You give** (drag items from your sheet, the quantity of a stack, money) and **You get**.
5. When one side changes their offer, the other must validate again. Both have validated: the trade is done. Closing the window cancels it.
6. A "Trade" card is whispered to the GM and both players.

## The rules

- An item holding other items (a full bag) must be emptied first.
- Received items go into the first container that accepts them. In a spatial grid with no room, the item waits **outside the grid** and the "Items without room" window opens: store it, drop it on the ground, throw it away or give it back.
- An item locked by an effect cannot be traded.

## Going further

To buy and sell with an NPC rather than between players: [Merchants](#marchands).
