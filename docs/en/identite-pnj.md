**What it's for:** immersion. A character the players haven't met yet is called "Unknown" on their side, until they introduce themselves.

**Example:** the players meet a merchant. They see "Unknown" on her token, in combat and in the chat. She introduces herself: the GM clicks the eye on her sheet, and her name appears everywhere, old messages included.

## How it works

- **Who is hidden**: any character without a player owner. A player's character is never hidden, and the GM always sees the real name.
- **The eye button** in the sheet header (GM): silhouette = hidden, eye = revealed.
- **Unlinked token**: each token is revealed on its own (revealing one guard doesn't reveal the other guards). **Linked token**: the whole sheet.

## The settings

**System settings**: **Hide NPC identities** (turn on or off) and **Name shown for an unknown** (empty = "Unknown"; for example "???").

## What is covered

The token label, the combat tracker and the [carousel](#initiative), sheet titles, the directory, the chat (the speaker, system cards, other modules' messages) and tooltips.

## Going further

This is immersion, not a secret: the real name reaches the player's computer, and they can find it in the browser console. For mysterious items: [Unidentified items](#identification).
