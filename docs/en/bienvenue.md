SBE (System Builder Engine) lets you build **your own game system** in Foundry: your sheets, your items, your rules, your rolls, your combat… **without writing code**. Everything is set up with the mouse.

## What you can do with SBE

- **Draw your character and item sheets** in the workshop: gauges, tabs, lists, inventory, talent trees…
- **Write your rules** with simple formulas (`strength + agility`) and dice rolls on click.
- **Automate**: buttons that chain steps (attack, heal, spend ammunition, apply an effect…).
- **Bring the scene to life**: chests, locked doors, traps, merchants, tracks on the ground, weather, calendar…
- **Dress it all up**: light and dark themes, chat messages, tooltips, pause banner.

## How to read this documentation

Every page follows the same plan:

1. **What it's for**, in one sentence.
2. **An example** you can reproduce as is.
3. **The options**, one by one.
4. **Going further**: advanced settings. If you are just starting, you can skip this part.

> The examples use neutral names (`hp`, `mana`, `strength`, `weight`…). Replace them with the ones of your game.

## Where to start

- New to SBE? Read [The basic ideas](#idees), then follow [Your first template in 10 minutes](#premier-modele).
- Want to see the system in action? Install [the Brumeval demo](#demo): a village ready to play.
- Looking for a specific setting? Type a word in the **search** box, top left.

## Help everywhere

- The **little book** in the header of an SBE window opens the page that explains it.
- On tool windows, the **Guided tour** button (the route icon, next to the book) shows the window step by step. All tours are also in **Settings → Tour Management**.
- The **"?"** next to a setting shows its explanation on hover.
- In **Game Settings**, tab **SBE — System Builder Engine**, the **Documentation** button reopens this manual. In this documentation, "system settings" always means that tab.
