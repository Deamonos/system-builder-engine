**What it's for:** bringing a group's characters together, and saving their state like in a video game to come back to it. Handy with several groups at the same table.

**Example:** a **Group** actor "The Companions". Drag the players' sheets into it, then **Save** in slot 1 before entering the crypt. Everything goes wrong: **Load**, and everything is back as it was.

## The group

**Create an actor** → **Group**. Drag the players' character sheets onto the group's sheet: portrait and name, a click opens the sheet, the cross removes it. The group's image changes with a click.

The group also serves as a **section** in the [quest journal](#quetes): its members follow it.

![The "The Companions" group: four members, three save slots and the auto save](img/en/groupe.webp)

## Saves

**3 slots** and **1 automatic**. A save keeps, for each member, their sheet, items and effects, the **world time** and the real date. Overwriting a slot asks for confirmation.

**Load** (with confirmation): the sheets go back to the saved moment, the world time too. What no longer exists is set aside (a deleted world item, a vanished effect, a key removed from the template), and the GM gets the list.

**Auto save**: only for the **active group** (one at a time, button on its sheet), at each new day and / or every N real minutes.

**Message and sound**: a message to the GM at each save and load; a sound heard by the GM only or by everyone.

## Going further

A key renamed after a save is not renamed in the save: its value will be set aside on load. Saves are for the GM only.
